How a booking works

We recommend booking three to six weeks ahead. Company events in December and January fill up faster, so message early if your date falls then.

  1. Check your date

    Send us your event date, venue and rough guest count. We'll reply with availability and a quote.

  2. Approve your strip

    We design a strip around your event and send a preview. Changes are free until you're happy.

  3. We set up

    We arrive about an hour early to set up the booth, lights and printer, then run it for the whole session.

  4. Guests take it home

    Each group gets printed strips on the spot.

Questions people usually ask

How much space does the booth need?

About 2.5m by 2.5m, near a power point. If your venue is tight, send us a photo of the spot and we'll tell you if it works.

Can the strip match our wedding colours or company branding?

Yes. Every strip is designed for your event. Send us your invitation, colour palette or logo and we'll build the design around it.

Do guests get digital copies?

Yes. Guests scan a QR code at the booth to download their photos onto their phones.

How far ahead should we book?

Three to six weeks is usually enough. For December, January and the Chinese New Year period, book as early as you can.

Do you charge extra for setup or transport?

Setup, teardown and transport within Singapore are included. Venues with special loading rules may need extra time, which we'll mention in your quote.

What print sizes do you offer?

Classic 2 x 6 inch photo strips, or larger 4R prints (4 x 6 inches). Strips fit three or four shots; 4R prints suit one big photo or a grid of four. Tell us which you prefer when you enquire.

How do I secure my date?

Your date is confirmed once we receive your deposit. Until then, the slot stays open to other enquiries, so pay the deposit early if your date is popular.

How much is the deposit, and when is the rest due?

We ask for a 50% deposit to secure your date. The balance is due 3 days before your event.

How can I pay?

PayNow or bank transfer. We'll send you an invoice with the payment details after you confirm your booking.

What if we need to cancel or postpone?

You can move your booking to another date once at no charge, as long as you tell us at least 14 days before the event and the new date is available. If you cancel, the deposit is non-refundable.

Can the booth be set up outdoors?

Yes, as long as the spot is sheltered from rain and direct sun and has a power point within a few metres. Let us know about outdoor setups when you enquire so we can plan for it.
